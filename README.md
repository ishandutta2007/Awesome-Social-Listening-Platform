# Top Social Listening Platform Tools Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Social Listening, Brand Monitoring, Sentiment Analysis, Media Intelligence & Conversation Tracking*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Social Listening**. These tools monitor brand mentions, track conversations across social media, news, forums and the web, analyze sentiment and trends, and deliver competitive and consumer intelligence.

**Examples** include Brandwatch, Meltwater, Sprinklr, Talkwalker, YouScan, Awario, Mention, Keyhole, Brand24, Synthesio, Digimind, and NetBase Quid (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting social listening, brand monitoring, sentiment analysis, and conversation tracking — ideal for privacy-conscious teams, researchers, startups, and developers who want full control without enterprise pricing.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Brandwatch](https://www.brandwatch.com/)**  
  Enterprise-grade consumer intelligence and social listening platform with advanced query building, massive historical data, audience insights, and deep analytics for large brands and research teams.

- **[Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/)**  
  Media intelligence and social listening suite strong in PR workflows, traditional media monitoring, journalist databases, and combined social + news coverage.

- **[Sprinklr](https://www.sprinklr.com/)**  
  Unified customer experience platform that includes robust social listening, engagement, and analytics capabilities for large enterprises.

- **[Talkwalker](https://www.talkwalker.com/)** (Hootsuite)  
  Large-scale social listening and media monitoring platform with broad multilingual coverage, visual listening, AI features (Blue Silk), and global source reach.

- **[YouScan](https://youscan.io/)**  
  AI-powered visual and social listening platform specializing in image recognition, brand mentions, and consumer insights.

- **[Awario](https://awario.com/)**  
  Accessible social listening and brand monitoring tool with Boolean search, influencer tracking, and competitive analysis at mid-market pricing.

- **[Mention](https://mention.com/)**  
  Real-time brand monitoring platform focused on mentions across social and web sources, with alerts, reporting, and share-of-voice tracking.

- **[Keyhole](https://keyhole.co/)**  
  Social listening and analytics platform strong in hashtag, campaign, and influencer tracking with real-time dashboards.

- **[Brand24](https://brand24.com/)**  
  Real-time brand monitoring and social listening tool popular with mid-sized teams for mention tracking, sentiment, and AI-assisted insights.

- **[Synthesio](https://www.synthesio.com/)** (Ipsos)  
  Consumer intelligence and social listening platform focused on behavioral analytics, trend detection, and strategic insights.

- **[Digimind](https://www.digimind.com/)**  
  Social listening and competitive intelligence platform offering monitoring, analytics, and market insights across multiple languages and sources.

- **[NetBase Quid](https://netbasequid.com/)**  
  AI-driven consumer and market intelligence platform combining social listening with advanced analytics and visualization.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Harken](https://github.com/VladUZH/harken)**  
  Self-hosted, open-source social listening tool that tracks mentions across Hacker News, Reddit, Mastodon, Bluesky, Stack Overflow, RSS, X, and YouTube, with local sentiment and theme analysis — no SaaS, no telemetry.

- **[OpenMagpie](https://github.com/obris-dev/openmagpie)**  
  Open-source, self-hostable social listening platform focused on scanning Reddit, Hacker News, and RSS for relevant conversations, with LLM-powered relevance scoring that can run fully locally.

- **[Open Social Media Monitoring](https://github.com/openstream/open-social-media-monitoring)**  
  Classic open-source social media monitoring suite for tracking keywords and collecting mentions from available sources via scheduled jobs.

- **[Watchman](https://github.com/Sotera/watchman)**  
  Open-source social-media event-detection system with utilities for large-scale data processing, clustering, and analysis of social streams.

- **[SONDY](https://github.com/AdrienGuille/SONDY)**  
  Open-source social media data mining software focused on event detection, topic tracking, and influence analysis with multiple algorithms and visualizations.

- **[Brandtrack and similar community platforms](https://github.com/RkayG/brandtrack)**  
  Open-source social listening projects that monitor brand mentions across social media, forums, and news with AI sentiment analysis and alerting features.

- **[Social Listening pipelines (Dagster, custom scrapers)](https://github.com/dagster-io/social-listening)**  
  Example and production-oriented open-source pipelines that collect mentions from social platforms and route them to Slack or dashboards for aggregated feeds.

- **[SMAT – Social Media Analysis Toolkit](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/)** (and related open tools)  
  Open-source toolkit for analyzing trends, hashtags, links, and conversations across platforms such as Twitter/X, Reddit, and imageboards.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Sentiment analysis libraries** (VADER, NLP.js, spaCy-based pipelines, transformer models) combined with custom scrapers or APIs.
- **Fediverse / Mastodon / Bluesky monitoring scripts** and self-hosted collectors.
- **Reddit, Hacker News, and forum-focused listeners** popular with developer and B2B teams.
- **OSINT frameworks** that include social data collection and basic sentiment/behavioral analysis.
- Many research and academic projects for event detection, topic modeling, and influence mapping on social streams.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Start with **Harken** or **OpenMagpie** for ready self-hosted listening, add open sentiment models or local LLMs (Ollama), pull additional sources via official APIs or ethical scrapers, store data in SQLite/Postgres, and build dashboards with Grafana, Metabase, or custom front-ends.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Social listening involves collecting and analyzing public data. Ensure compliance with platform terms of service, privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), and ethical guidelines. Unauthorized scraping or misuse of personal data can have legal consequences.
- Self-hosted open-source solutions require ongoing maintenance of data pipelines, API keys, rate limits, and storage. Enterprise platforms typically offer far broader historical data and source coverage.
